Questions we get

Not here? Call or message us.

Do you give fixed prices?

Yes. We issue an itemised quotation before work starts, and any change is priced and approved in writing first.

How long does a project take?

It depends on size and approvals. We give you a written programme with dates in the quotation.

Can you handle permits and approvals?

Yes. We prepare drawings and submit to the relevant authorities on your behalf.

Can we stay in the building during works?

For many renovations, yes. We plan noisy work and access routes around your routine.

What happens after handover?

We fix defects found at inspection and stay available for questions about the works.
